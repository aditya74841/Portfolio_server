Project: POS System - BlackWater Coffee

A comprehensive point-of-sale system that transformed retail operations for a growing coffee chain, focusing on efficiency and accuracy in daily operations.

Key Achievements:
- Processing 500+ daily transactions with 99.9% uptime
- Reduced checkout time by 40% (from 3.5 to 2.1 minutes)
- Improved inventory accuracy from 75% to 95%
- Decreased stock discrepancies by 30%
- Accelerated order fulfillment by 25% through Kitchen Display System

Technical Implementation:
- Full-stack JavaScript architecture
- Real-time order management system
- Inventory tracking and analytics
- Integration with kitchen display systems
- Automated reporting and analytics

Business Impact:
- Streamlined daily operations
- Improved customer satisfaction through faster service
- Better inventory management and reduced waste
- Enhanced staff efficiency and reduced training time
